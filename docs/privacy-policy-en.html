ShadeSide Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026
Last Modified: October 20, 2026
Version: 1.2

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application "ShadeSide" (the "App") collects, uses, and protects information related to your use of the App. The developer of this App values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

1. Personal Information We Collect Directly

This App does not directly collect any personal information from users.

  • No sign-up or account creation feature
  • No requests for identifying information such as name, phone number, or email
  • No request for access to your device's location (GPS)
  • No requests for sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Photo library access is requested only when you choose to save or share a game image, and only to save that image

2. Information Stored on Your Device

For your convenience, this App stores the following information only on your device. This information is never sent to any external server and is deleted when the App is uninstalled.

Stored ItemPurpose
List of favorite stadiumsPrioritized display on the home screen
List of favorite teamsAutomatic alerts for your team's games
Alert timing settings (1/3/6/12/24 hours)User notification preferences
Alert on/off statusPer-game alert management
Cached game scheduleSupport for offline use

Cookies and similar technologies on your device

This App is a mobile app and does not use browser cookies itself, but it stores information on your device and accesses information from it (as do the third-party services we use) through similar technologies:

  • Local storage on your device (AsyncStorage / app preferences): your favorites, alert settings and cached game data (see the table above). It stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Push notification token and installation identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging): stored on your device and processed by Google/Apple to deliver notifications (Section 3.3).
  • Advertising identifier and similar identifiers (Google AdMob): the AdMob SDK may access and store identifiers on your device (Section 3.1). On iOS only non-personalized ads are requested.
  • Crash-report data (Sentry): technical information about errors, device model and OS (Section 3.4).
  • YouTube: the App does not embed YouTube players and does not place YouTube cookies. When you tap a video link, YouTube opens in the YouTube app or your browser, where YouTube/Google may use cookies and similar technologies under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can control these: clear the App's data or uninstall it, reset or limit your advertising identifier in device settings, turn off notifications, and manage cookies in your browser or YouTube/Google account settings. The App's introduction web page does not use tracking cookies.

3. Use of Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain information on their own.

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

This App uses Google AdMob to display banner and native ads. AdMob may automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language settings)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Ad impression and interaction records

On iOS, this App requests only non-personalized ads and does not display the App Tracking Transparency prompt; it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

For more details: Google Privacy Policy

3.2 External API Calls (Only Stadium Location Is Sent)

This App calls the following APIs to retrieve game schedules and weather information. No personal information about the user is sent.

ServiceData SentPurpose
Naver Sports API None (only public schedule data is retrieved) KBO/K League game schedules
Open-Meteo Stadium coordinates (not the user's location) Stadium-specific weather forecast

When these APIs are called, the device's IP address may be transmitted to the respective servers, but no identifiable personal information is sent.

3.3 Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)

This App uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications (via Apple Push Notification service on iOS). To deliver notifications, FCM assigns your device a push token and an installation identifier. The App also subscribes your device to notification topics that correspond to the teams, games, players, and language you choose (for example, a favorite team's final-score alert). These topics are not linked to your name, email, or any account, because this App has no accounts. You can turn off notifications in your device settings, or remove a favorite in the App to unsubscribe from its topic. For more details: Firebase Privacy and Security.

3.4 Sentry (Crash and Performance Reporting)

This App uses Sentry to receive crash reports and basic performance measurements so we can fix problems. A report contains technical information such as the error and stack trace, device model, OS version, and App version. The App is configured not to send personal information (no name, email, or account identifiers). For more details: Sentry Privacy Policy.

3.5 YouTube API Services

This App uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API) to link match-highlight and cheer-song videos. Calls are made only from our servers; no personal information or Google account information of App users is sent to or collected from YouTube. We store only video IDs and titles. When you open a video you are taken to YouTube (app or browser), where the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. We do not store YouTube user data, so there is no such data to delete. You can revoke any access granted to your Google account in the Google security settings. We refresh the stored video information (ID and title) from the YouTube API at least every 7 days and remove it when the video is no longer available, so no YouTube API data is kept for more than 30 days without being refreshed. By using the highlight and cheer-song links you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

4. Notifications

This App sends alerts for teams, games, and players you choose. Notifications are delivered as push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging (see Section 3.3), so a push token for your device is processed by Google and Apple for that purpose. No personal information is required. You can change or turn off your alert settings at any time.
