Shadow Angle (ShadowStadium) Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026
Last Modified: June 1, 2026
Version: 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application "Shadow Angle (ShadowStadium)" (the "App") collects, uses, and protects information related to your use of the App. The developer of this App values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

1. Personal Information We Collect Directly

This App does not directly collect any personal information from users.

  • No sign-up or account creation feature
  • No requests for identifying information such as name, phone number, or email
  • No request for access to your device's location (GPS)
  • No requests for sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or storage

2. Information Stored on Your Device

For your convenience, this App stores the following information only on your device. This information is never sent to any external server and is deleted when the App is uninstalled.

Stored ItemPurpose
List of favorite stadiumsPrioritized display on the home screen
List of favorite teamsAutomatic alerts for your team's games
Alert timing settings (1/3/6/12/24 hours)User notification preferences
Alert on/off statusPer-game alert management
Cached game scheduleSupport for offline use

3. Use of Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain information on their own.

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

This App uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. AdMob may automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language settings)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Ad impression and interaction records

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

For more details: Google Privacy Policy

3.2 External API Calls (Only Stadium Location Is Sent)

This App calls the following APIs to retrieve game schedules and weather information. No personal information about the user is sent.

ServiceData SentPurpose
Naver Sports API None (only public schedule data is retrieved) KBO/K League game schedules
Open-Meteo Stadium coordinates (not the user's location) Stadium-specific weather forecast

When these APIs are called, the device's IP address may be transmitted to the respective servers, but no identifiable personal information is sent.

4. Notifications

This App sends alerts for game times that you configure. All notifications are processed locally on your device, and this App does not receive push notifications from an external server. You can change or turn off your alert settings at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights:

  • Immediately delete all information stored on your device by uninstalling the App
  • Change notification permissions in your device settings
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
