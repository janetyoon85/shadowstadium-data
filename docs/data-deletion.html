App: ShadeSide (Google Play package com.shadowstadium.app)

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)
Open a game → "Cheer" chat → long-press your own message → Delete. Your message is removed immediately.
Option 2 — Email request for all your data (handled within 7 business days)
Email ybbbenny@gmail.com with the subject "ShadeSide data deletion request" and include:
· The nickname you used
· The game (team names and date) where you posted
We will delete everything listed below and reply to you. If you reinstalled the app, your previous anonymous ID cannot be shown in the app, so we locate your posts by nickname and game.
